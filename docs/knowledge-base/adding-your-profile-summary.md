# Adding Your Profile Summary

The profile summary is a short paragraph about your experience and strengths that seeds every CV you build — it's a starting point, not something that gets silently overwritten each time.

## Steps

1. Go to **CV components** (Documents menu → "Manage CV components," or "Manage resume components" for US users).
2. At the top of the page, under "Profile summary," type or edit your paragraph in the text box.
3. Click **Save**.

## Notes

- The profile summary is part of the same CV components library as skills, experience, education, certifications, and custom sections.
- Instead of writing it by hand, it can also be filled in automatically by uploading an existing CV/resume and letting the app extract a summary from it for review before saving.
