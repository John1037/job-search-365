# Adding Education Entries

On the **CV components** page, under "Education," click **Add education**.

## Fields

- **Educational establishment** * (e.g. "University of Manchester")
- **Educational level** * (e.g. "BSc, A-level, GCSE")
- **Subject** (optional, e.g. "Mathematics")
- **Grade** (optional, e.g. "Second Class, B, A*") alongside **Year qualified** * (required)
- **Additional information** — a bullet-point list, add more with **Add detail**, each with ↑ / ↓ reorder and ✕ remove controls

Click **Save** (or **Cancel**). Entries list as "{Level} — {Establishment}" with the qualification year and grade beneath, plus **Edit** / **Delete** buttons (delete asks you to confirm).

## Notes

- Entries are automatically ordered by qualification year, most recent first.
