# Generating a Cover Letter

Drafts a cover letter for one specific job, written from that job's description and the CV connected to it. You review and edit the draft before anything is saved.

## Requirements

- The job's status must be "Interested." Once you've moved past that, the button disappears.
- The job needs a job description, and a connected CV that's a PDF file (not a Word doc). If either is missing, the **Suggest cover letter** button is disabled and a hint says which one to fix: "Add a job description to enable this," "Connect a CV to enable the cover letter suggestion," or "Connect a PDF CV to enable the cover letter suggestion."

Any unsaved edits on the job's detail page (for example, a description you've just pasted in) are saved automatically when you click **Suggest cover letter**, so the draft always uses what's on screen.

## Steps

1. On the job's detail page, click **Suggest cover letter**.
2. Optionally, fill in **Anything specific you want included?** with anything the letter should mention that your CV doesn't, such as your notice period, that you're relocating to the area, or why this employer appeals to you. Leave it blank to let the draft work from your CV and the job description alone.
3. Click **Generate**. While it's working you'll see "Drafting your cover letter…".
4. Edit the draft directly in the text box. It's both the preview and the place to make changes, so just type.
5. Click **Save as cover letter** (shows "Saving…"), or **Cancel** to discard it.

## What the draft looks like

- **Length:** 3-4 paragraphs. The draft focuses on a handful of your strongest, most relevant points rather than trying to cover your whole CV.
- **Wording:** The draft is written in your own voice and paraphrases rather than copying sentences from your CV or the job description.
- **Your notes:** Anything you entered in the optional box is worked into the letter naturally, not pasted in word for word or added as a separate list.
- **Only real details:** The draft only uses what's in your CV and your notes. If something isn't there, the letter writes around it instead of inventing details, so check that it hasn't skipped anything important.
- **Plain text:** No headings, formatting, subject line or placeholder brackets.

## What happens on save

Two files are added to your cover-letter document library:

- a `.txt` copy, which is easy to edit later
- a `.pdf` version

The PDF is automatically connected to the job as its cover letter, so you don't need a separate step to attach it.
