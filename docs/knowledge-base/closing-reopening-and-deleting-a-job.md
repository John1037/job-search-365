# Closing, Reopening, and Deleting a Job

## Closing a job

"Closed" just removes a job from your main open-jobs list — it doesn't delete anything.

- From the jobs list: click **Close** next to the job — no confirmation needed.
- From a job's own detail page: click **Close** (next to Delete, near the bottom of the editable form).
- Automatically: logging an **Unsuccessful** event closes the job for you.

A closed job moves to the **closed jobs list** (**Jobs → View closed jobs**, or `/jobs/closed`).

## Reopening a job

- From the closed jobs list: click **Reopen** next to the job.
- From the job's own detail page (once closed, the same button now reads **Reopen**): click it.
- Automatically: deleting an Unsuccessful event reopens the job.

## Deleting a job

This is permanent — unlike closing, it cannot be undone.

- From either jobs list, or from the job's own detail page, click **Delete**.
- Confirm: "Delete '{title}' at '{employer}'? This can't be undone." → click **Delete** to confirm.
