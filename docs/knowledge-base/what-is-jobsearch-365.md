# What Is JobSearch 365

JobSearch 365 is a job-application tracker. It replaces a spreadsheet for people actively job hunting: log every job you're interested in or have applied to, track its status through interviews and offers, keep a reusable library of CV/resume building blocks (skills, experience, education, certifications), generate a tailored CV and cover letter for a specific job in a few clicks, store the documents you send out, and optionally connect Gmail so the app can spot application-related emails (interview invites, rejections, offers) and suggest matching status updates.

For US-based users the app uses "resume" wording throughout instead of "CV" — the underlying feature is identical either way.

## Main areas of the app

- **Dashboard** (`/main`) — an at-a-glance view of upcoming alerts, current applications, and jobs you're interested in.
- **Jobs** — add a job (manually or by importing a job posting URL), track its status via logged events (Applied, Interview scheduled, Offer received, etc.), and manage the full list with search/filter/sort.
- **CV components** — your reusable library: profile summary, skills, experience, education, certifications, and custom sections. A CV build for a specific job draws from this library rather than starting from scratch each time.
- **Documents** — upload and store CVs, cover letters, certificates, and other files; connect specific documents to specific jobs.
- **Inbox** — connect a Gmail account (read-only) so the app can scan for application-related emails and suggest status updates for you to review and confirm.
- **Profile & Settings** — contact details (including a separate email address used specifically on CVs/applications), theme, and alert preferences.

New users are shown a short getting-started checklist covering completing their profile, adding CV components, and adding their first job.
