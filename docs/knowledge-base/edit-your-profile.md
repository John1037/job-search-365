# Edit Your Profile

Reached via the profile menu (top right) → **Edit profile**, or directly at `/profile`.

## Fields on this page

- **Photo** — click **Upload photo** (or **Change photo** if one's already set) to pick an image from your device. This photo can optionally appear on your CV.
- **Full legal name** — shown as "This will be visible to potential employers."
- **Short-form name** — how the app addresses you (e.g. on the dashboard greeting).
- **Account email address** — your login email, shown but not directly editable here. Click **Change** to open a dialog where you enter and confirm a new address, then click "Send confirmation" — the change only takes effect once you follow the confirmation link sent to the new address.
- **Email address for applications** — a separate address used on your CVs and anywhere else you're contacted about a job. This is deliberately independent of your login email. If you haven't set one yet, it starts pre-filled with your account email, but you can change it to any address you want — for example if you use a different inbox for job hunting. It's validated as a proper email address format when you save; if it's invalid, nothing on the page is saved and an error appears directly under this field.
- **Phone number** — country code selector plus number.
- **Location (city)**
- **Country** — a dropdown of countries; selecting a US-based country switches the app's wording from "CV" to "resume" throughout.
- **LinkedIn profile**, **GitHub account**, **Portfolio**, **Website** — each has an "Open" link that appears once you've typed a URL, so you can check it's correct.

## Saving

Click **Save** at the bottom of the form. All fields save together in one action — there's no per-field save.

If the "Email address for applications" field fails validation, the whole save is blocked (nothing is saved, not even your other changes) and an error message appears under that field specifically. Fix it and click Save again.

## Deleting your account

At the bottom of the page, under "Danger Zone," is **Delete my account** — this permanently deletes the account and all its data after typing DELETE to confirm; it cannot be undone.
