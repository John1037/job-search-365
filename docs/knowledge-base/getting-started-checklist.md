# Getting Started Checklist (Welcome Screen)

New (or not-yet-fully-set-up) users see a "Welcome to JobSearch 365" screen with three suggested next steps, each shown as a card:

1. **Complete your profile** → links to the Edit profile page. Counted complete once full name, short-form name, location, and country are all filled in.
2. **Add your CV components** → links to the CV components page. Counted complete once there's a profile summary, at least one skill, and at least one of: an experience entry, an education entry, or a custom section.
3. **Add a job** → links to the jobs list. Counted complete the first time a job has ever been added — even if every job is later deleted, this stays marked complete.

A completed card shows a green checkmark.

## When this screen appears

It shows at the start of a session (logging in, or a fresh browser tab) whenever at least one of the three steps is still incomplete. Once all three are complete, you're taken straight to your dashboard instead.

## Skipping it

- Click **Head to your dashboard** to go straight to `/main`.
- Check **Do not show this screen again** before clicking a card or the dashboard button, and it won't appear again at the start of future sessions — even if some steps are still incomplete. (Checking the box alone does nothing until you then click through.)
