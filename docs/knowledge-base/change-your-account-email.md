# Change Your Account Email

This changes your **login** email — the address you use to sign in. It's separate from the "email address for applications" field on your profile, which is what appears on CVs and job applications instead and is edited directly on the profile page without needing a confirmation email.

## Steps

1. Go to **Edit profile** and click **Change** next to your account email.
2. Enter the **New email address**.
3. Enter it again in **Confirm new email address**.
4. Click **Send confirmation**.
5. You'll see: "Check your new email inbox to confirm the change. Your email address will update once confirmed." Click **Close**.
6. Open the confirmation email sent to the new address and follow the link to complete the change.

## Errors you might see

- "Email addresses do not match." — the two fields you typed don't match; re-enter them.
- "That is already your current email address." — you entered your existing email as the "new" one.

## Notes

- Your login email doesn't actually change until you confirm it via the emailed link — until then, keep logging in with your old email.
