# Log In

## Steps

1. From the landing page, click **Log in**.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **Log in**.

If your password isn't accepted and you're confident it's correct, try typing it manually rather than relying on your browser's saved/autofilled password — saved passwords are stored per website address, so a password saved for a slightly different address (e.g. a local/test version of the site) will not work on the live site, and vice versa.

## Forgot your password?

1. In the login dialog, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your account email address.
3. Click **Send reset link**.
4. Check your email for a password reset link and follow it — it opens a "Set a new password" page in the app where a new password can be entered and confirmed.

## Notes

- The account/login email can be viewed and changed from the Edit profile page once logged in.
