# Understanding Your Dashboard

Your dashboard (`/main`) is the first thing you see after logging in, greeting you by name. It has four sections.

## Alerts

Shows anything happening soon, within a configurable window (set on the Settings page, "Show alerts for events in the next"; defaults to 30 days). Two kinds of alert appear here:

- **Upcoming events** — any logged event (like an interview) whose date falls within your alert window. Click it to go straight to that job.
- **"Assumed unsuccessful" warnings** — if a job is sitting at "Application acknowledged" status and the employer's expected response date has passed with nothing else logged, an alert appears 7 days after that date warning the job will be treated as unsuccessful. A **Close job** button on the alert lets you close it directly from here.

If nothing's coming up, you'll see "Nothing happening in next {N} days."

## Current applications

Every open (not closed) job whose status is anything other than "Interested" — i.e. jobs you've actually applied to or are further along with. Sorted newest-logged first.

## Current interested jobs

Every open job still at "Interested" status — jobs you're considering but haven't applied to yet.

Both of these sections show an **Add a job** link when empty.

## Potential jobs

Currently a placeholder section ("Coming soon.") — no functionality yet.
