# Navigating the App

Once logged in, every page shares the same top navigation bar.

## Top bar, left to right

- **Logo** (top left) — click it to return to the dashboard (`/main`).
- **Documents** menu (shown as "Docs" on narrow screens) — a dropdown with:
  - "Manage Resumes" (US users) / "Manage CVs" (everyone else) — your uploaded CV files
  - "Manage cover letters"
  - "Manage certificates"
  - "Manage other documents"
  - "Manage resume components" (US) / "Manage CV components" (everyone else) — your reusable library of skills, experience, education, etc.
- **Jobs** menu — a dropdown with:
  - "Add a job" — opens the add-a-job dialog directly, without navigating away
  - "Manage jobs" — the full open-jobs list
  - "View closed jobs" — jobs you've closed
  - "Inbox" — Gmail-based application-update scanning
- **Profile menu** (circular avatar or person icon, top right) — click it to open a dropdown with:
  - "Edit profile"
  - "Settings"
  - "Log out"

Click anywhere outside an open dropdown to close it.

## Getting back to the dashboard

Most individual pages (Manage jobs, Job detail, CV components, Documents, Settings) have a "Back to home" or "Back to jobs" button at the bottom as a shortcut, in addition to clicking the logo.
