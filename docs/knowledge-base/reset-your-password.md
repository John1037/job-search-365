# Reset Your Password

## Requesting a reset

1. On the login dialog, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your account email address and click **Send reset link**.
3. You'll see: "Check your email for a password reset link."

## Completing the reset

1. Open the password reset email and click the link — it takes you to a "Set a new password" page in the app.
2. Enter a **New password** and **Confirm password**.
3. Click **Update password**.
4. On success you'll see "Password updated. Redirecting…" and be taken to your dashboard automatically.

## Notes

- If passwords don't match, you'll see "Passwords do not match." — re-enter them.
- After resetting, type the new password manually the first time you log in rather than relying purely on autofill, to make sure your browser saves the correct, current password for this site going forward.
