# Delete Your Account

This permanently deletes your account and all of your data — jobs, documents, CV components, everything. This cannot be undone.

## Steps

1. Go to **Edit profile**, scroll to the "Danger Zone" section.
2. Click **Delete my account**.
3. Read the warning: "This action is not reversible, ALL of your data will be deleted, and we won't be able to recover it. Proceed?"
4. Type `DELETE` (exactly, in capitals) into the confirmation box.
5. Click **Delete my account**. The button stays disabled until you've typed `DELETE` correctly.

If you change your mind at any point before confirming, click **Go back**.

## Notes

- This also revokes any connected Gmail access as part of the deletion.
- There's no partial/soft delete — once confirmed, everything is gone immediately.
