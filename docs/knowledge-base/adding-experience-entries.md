# Adding Experience Entries

On the **CV components** page, under "Experience," click **Add experience** to open the entry form.

## Fields

- **Job title** * and **Employer** * (required)
- **Location** (optional)
- **Start year** * (required) and **Start month** (optional)
- **I currently work here** — a checkbox; if checked, no end date is needed
- **End year** / **End month** (optional) — only if not marked as current
- **Achievements / responsibilities** — a bullet-point list. Type a bullet, and use **Add bullet** to add more. Each bullet row has ↑ / ↓ buttons to reorder it and a ✕ to remove it.

Click **Save** to add it (or **Cancel** to discard). Once saved, the entry appears in your Experience list showing "{Job title} — {Employer}" with the date range beneath it, along with **Edit** and **Delete** buttons.

## Editing or deleting

- Click **Edit** on any entry to reopen the same form pre-filled.
- Click **Delete** to remove it — you'll be asked to confirm ("Delete '{Job title} — {Employer}'? This can't be undone.").

## Notes

- Entries are listed automatically with your current role (or most recent end date) first — there's no manual reordering of whole entries, only of the bullet points within one entry.
- When generating a CV for a specific job, the user chooses how many of the most recent roles get shown in full detail versus compacted into a one-line summary, so it's worth keeping every role's achievements/responsibilities filled in even for older roles.
