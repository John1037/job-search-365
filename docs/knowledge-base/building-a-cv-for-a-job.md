# Building a CV for a Job

Builds a CV tailored to one specific job, using your CV components library rather than starting from scratch. The build picks and orders your existing content for the job. It doesn't make up skills or experience, and you review and edit everything before saving.

## Requirements

- The job's status must be "Interested." Once you've moved past that, the button disappears.
- The job needs a **Job description** on its detail page. Without one, the button is disabled with the hint "Add a job description to enable this."

Any unsaved edits on the job's detail page are saved automatically when you click the button, so the build always uses what's on screen.

## Starting

On the job's detail page, click **Build CV** (or **Build resume** for US users).

## Stage 1: Configure

- **Show full detail for the most recent [N] roles/years.** Enter a number and choose roles or years. This controls how much of your Experience section appears in full, with all its bullets. Roles outside that window are shortened to a single summary line each, under an "Earlier Career" heading.
- **Let AI lightly reword wording to match this job.** Optional and off by default. When it's on, your profile paragraph and experience bullets can be rephrased to use the job description's own language. It only changes the wording: nothing is added, removed, exaggerated or changed about what you actually did. When it's off, your bullets appear exactly as you saved them.

Click **Build** (shows "Building your CV…") or **Cancel** to back out.

## Stage 2: Suggested skills (only shown sometimes)

If the job description names skills that aren't in your CV components library yet, you'll see them as a list before the review stage.

- Select any skills you **genuinely have**, then click **Add [N] skills and rebuild**. They're saved to your permanent skills library, and the CV is rebuilt so they can be included properly.
- Click **Skip** to go to the review stage without adding any.

Only add skills you really have. Adding one just to match the job description is the kind of claim that falls apart in an interview. This stage appears at most once per build.

## Stage 3: Review

Every section the build produced is shown and can be edited: Profile, Skills, Experience, Earlier Career, Education, Certifications, any custom sections, and a Links section if you've set a LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio or website link on your profile.

- **Edit any text directly:** the profile paragraph, skills, bullet points, education and certification details, and custom section content.
- **Reorder** skills, bullets and detail lines by dragging the handle next to each item. This works with a mouse or with your finger on a phone or tablet.
- **Remove** a single item with its remove button, a whole experience, education or certification entry with its **Remove** button, or a whole custom or Links section with **Remove section**.
- **Add skill** opens a picker:
  - It lists skills from your library that weren't included. Click one to add it.
  - You can also type a new skill and click **Add**. It's added to this CV and saved to your permanent skills library. If it matches a skill you already have, the existing one is reused instead.
- **Add bullet** (on each experience entry) opens a picker:
  - It lists that role's saved bullets that weren't included. Click one to add it.
  - Click **Write a new bullet** for a one-off line that's used only in this CV.
- **Add detail** adds a line to an education or certification entry.

If any automatic selection step didn't work as expected, a notice above the review explains what happened. For example, all skills may have been included instead of a tailored set. Check those parts yourself, or cancel and build again.

Click **Continue** to choose a template, or **Cancel** to discard the whole build.

## Stage 4: Template

- **Template:** choose from 8 layouts: Classic, Sidebar, Sidebar Right, Compact, Bold, Compact Sidebar, Vivid and Vivid Sidebar. Sidebar layouts put skills, education and certifications in a side column. The "Vivid" layouts use bolder color blocks.
- **Color:** each template offers six color swatches: Navy (Slate on sidebar layouts), Charcoal, Forest (Sage on sidebar layouts), Burgundy, Plum and Ink. Your chosen color stays selected when you switch templates, if the new template offers it.
- **Font:** a Sans-serif / Serif toggle.
- **Include profile photo:** a checkbox, shown only if the chosen template supports a photo and you've uploaded one to your profile.

Click **Preview** to see the actual PDF in the dialog. While the preview is open, it updates as you change the template, color, font or photo; click **Close Preview** to hide it. If your browser can't show it inline (common on phones), use the **Open preview in a new tab** link.

Click **Back** to return to the review stage, which closes any open preview. Click **Save as CV** (or **Save as resume**) to finish. This saves the PDF to your document library and connects it to the job automatically, so you don't need to attach it separately.

## What it draws from

The build uses your saved CV components library: your profile summary, skills, experience, education, certifications and custom sections. It also adds a Links section for any LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio or website link on your profile. Education is always included in full, in date order. The other sections are selected and ordered based on the job description.
