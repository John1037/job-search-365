# Building a CV for a Job

Generates a CV tailored to one specific job, drawing from your CV components library rather than starting from scratch.

## Requirements

- The job's status must be "Interested" (once you've moved past that, this button disappears).
- The job needs a **Job description** filled in on its detail page — without one, the button is disabled with a hint: "Add a job description to enable this."

## Starting

On the job's detail page, click **Build CV** (or **Build resume** for US users).

## Stage 1 — Configure

Set "Show full detail for the most recent [N] roles/years" — a number plus a roles-or-years toggle. This controls how much of your Experience section is shown in full (bullets and all) versus compacted into a single summary line for older roles. Click **Build** (shows "Building…") to have it select and arrange content from your library based on the job description, or **Cancel** to back out.

## Stage 2 — Review

Every section the build produced (Profile, Skills, Experience, Education, Certifications, any custom sections, and a Links section if you have LinkedIn/GitHub/portfolio/website set) is shown editable:

- Edit any text directly (profile paragraph, bullet points, custom section content, etc.).
- Skills: click **Add skill** to add one from your library that wasn't automatically included, or type a brand-new one — either way it's also added to your permanent skills library.
- Remove an entire experience/education/certification entry with its own **Remove** button, or remove a whole custom/links section with **Remove section**.

Click **Continue** to move to template selection, or **Cancel** to discard the whole build.

## Stage 3 — Template

- **Template** — choose from 8 layouts: Classic, Sidebar, Sidebar Right, Compact, Bold, Compact Sidebar, Vivid, Vivid Sidebar (sidebar layouts put skills/education/certifications in a side column; "Vivid" ones use bolder color-block styling).
- **Color** — each template offers six color swatches (most include Navy/Slate, Charcoal, Forest/Sage, Burgundy, Plum, and Ink).
- **Font** — a Sans-serif / Serif toggle, top of the dialog.
- **Include profile photo** — a checkbox, only shown if the chosen template supports a photo and a photo has been uploaded on the user's profile.

Click **Preview** to render and view the actual PDF right there (or **Close Preview** to hide it) — if it can't render inline in your browser, an **Open preview in a new tab** link appears instead. Click **Back** to return to the review stage (this clears any open preview), or **Save as CV** / **Save as resume** (shows "Saving…") to finish — this saves the PDF to the document library and connects it to the job automatically, so there's no separate step needed to attach it afterward.

## What it draws from

The build pulls from the same profile summary, skills, experience, education, certifications, and custom sections a user has built up as their CV components library, plus a Links section listing any LinkedIn/GitHub/portfolio/website they've set on their profile.
