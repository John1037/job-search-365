# Connecting Documents to a Job

A job can have up to six documents attached: a CV, a cover letter, a certificate, and three general "other document" slots.

## Connecting a document

1. On the job's detail page, click **Connect document** (only shown while the job's status is "Interested," and only once at least one slot is still free).
2. Choose a category — CV, Cover letter, Certificate, or Other document (a category shows "All slots full" and is disabled once its slot(s) are used).
3. Pick from your already-uploaded documents in that category (each shows a **Select** button; your default document, if any, is marked). If you haven't uploaded anything in that category yet, you'll see "No {category}s uploaded yet. Upload one from the Documents menu first." — documents are uploaded from the relevant category page under the top nav's Documents menu, not from this dialog.

## Viewing what's connected

Click **Manage documents** on the job's detail page (only shown once at least one document is connected) to see everything currently attached, with **View** and **Download** buttons for each, and a **Disconnect** button (only available while the job is still at "Interested" status) to remove it from that slot without deleting the document itself from your library.

## Notes

- Building a CV or generating a cover letter for the job connects the result to the job automatically once saved — there's no need to use Connect document for those.
- Disconnecting only unlinks the document from this job; it stays in your document library.
