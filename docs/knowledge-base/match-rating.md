# Match Rating

A 0–100 score showing how well your CV components fit a specific job's description — calculated automatically, with no need to build a CV first just to see how close a fit it is.

## How it's calculated

Whenever a job's description is added or changed, your full CV components library (profile summary, skills, experience, education, certifications, and custom sections) is compared against it and scored from 0 (no meaningful overlap) to 100 (an excellent match). The score weighs core requirements and responsibilities more heavily than simple keyword overlap — missing a minor detail while being strong on the main requirements scores higher than the reverse.

A job with no description yet shows no match rating.

## Where it shows

- **Manage jobs** (and the jobs list elsewhere) — a small circle at the top-right of the job card's title, showing the score. The ring color shades from red (below 30) through orange and yellow to green (above 70), with the number always shown in white on a black center. If the job is also marked Preferred or Favorite, its star rating shows separately, right-aligned on the salary/location line below.
- **Job detail page** — the same circle, right-aligned on the job title row. If the job also has a star rating, the stars sit immediately to its left.

## Keeping it up to date

The score recalculates automatically only when the job's description itself changes — editing your CV components (adding a skill, a bullet point, a new role, etc.) doesn't retrigger it on its own. To refresh scores after updating your CV, go to **CV components** and click **Recalculate match scores** (bottom right, next to Back to home). This recalculates every open job that has a description and reports how many were updated.

## Sorting by match score

On **Manage jobs**, open **Sort by** and choose **Match score** as a sort field, same as any other sort option (ascending or descending, combinable with up to two other sort levels). Jobs without a score yet always sort last.
